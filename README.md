# Twixter.Store

**[twixter.store](https://twixter.store)**: a social-style video platform where people discover content for free, unlock premium videos with credits, or subscribe for full access.

---

## What it is

Twixter feels like a social feed. You scroll posts, follow tags, and bookmark what you like. Premium videos stay locked until you buy them with credits or hold a membership. Free previews let people see the content before they pay.

The platform has two content categories:

- **For you (Light)**: general content, open to everyone.
- **Premium (Dark)**: adult (NSFW) content, available only to accounts that have the right permission.

---

## Features

### Discover

- **Home feed**: *For you* and *Following* timelines with free and premium posts mixed together.
- **Explore**: search all content and sort it by view count, upload date, or free-only.
- **Tags**: tag pages collect related posts, and a *Trending* sidebar shows what's popular.
- **Slideshow**: a TikTok-style vertical player. Each video plays a free 60-second preview from its middle, then moves on to the next one automatically.
- **GIF previews**: every video gets an animated preview, so you can tell what it is before you unlock it.

### Watch and collect

- **Unlock with credits**: each premium post has a credit price. Buy it once and it's yours.
- **Library**: every video you've purchased, in one place.
- **Bookmarks**: save posts to come back to.
- **Watch progress**: playback resumes where you left off.
- **Share premium links**: send someone a link to a premium post. They can sign up straight from it.

### Pay your way

- **Credits**: buy credit packages and spend them on individual videos or chat.
- **Membership**: 1-, 3-, or 6-month plans that unlock all content.
- **Checkout**: pay with Stripe or PayPal. Credits land in your wallet as soon as the payment is confirmed.

### Persona Chat

- An AI companion (NSFW) that you name and give a personality, and it stays in character.
- Each message costs 6.99 credits. Only premium accounts can use it.

### Grow with referrals

- Every profile has a shareable referral link.
- You earn credits whenever someone signs up with your code. You can share the link once per month.
- New users get free credits when they sign up.

### For creators and admins

- **Bulk upload**: upload up to 10 videos at once, each with its own name, description, credit price, category, and tags. Videos are transcoded automatically for smooth adaptive streaming, and their previews are generated for you.
- **Post management**: edit or delete published content.
- **Video analytics**: views, plays, watch time, and top content for any date range.
- **Revenue analytics**: content revenue (credits spent), credit sales (real money), and total revenue.
- Access depends on **permissions**, not account type.

### Accounts

- Email sign-up and sign-in, plus password reset.
- At sign-up, you choose whether you prefer general or premium content.
- Available in **English** and **中文**.
